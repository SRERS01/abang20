To check or verify your proxy settings on a Xiaomi device running MIUI or HyperOS, you can look directly into your active connection settings or use a web verification tool. [1, 2] 
Here is how to check your proxy configuration step-by-step:
## Method 1: Check Wi-Fi Proxy Settings

   1. Open the Settings app on your Xiaomi phone. [1] 
   2. Tap on Wi-Fi. [1] 
   3. Find the network you are currently connected to and tap the arrow icon (>) or info button next to it. [1, 3] 
   4. Scroll down and tap on Proxy. [1, 3] 
   5. Here you can see your status:
   * None: No proxy is active.
      * Manual: You will see the configured proxy hostname and port number.
      * Proxy Auto-Config: You will see a PAC URL script. [1, 3] 
   
## Method 2: Check Mobile Data Proxy Settings
If you are using mobile data, proxy configurations are tied to your Access Point Name (APN). [4] 

   1. Go to Settings > SIM cards & mobile networks.
   2. Select your active SIM card, then tap on Access Point Names (APN).
   3. Tap the arrow or menu icon next to your active APN to edit/view details.
   4. Look at the Proxy and Port fields to see if any values are entered. [4] 

## Method 3: Verify if the Proxy is Working
Even if a proxy is configured in your settings, it might not be functioning properly. To test it:

   1. Open Google Chrome or any browser on your device.
   2. Navigate to a public IP checker website like [WhatIsMyIP](https://www.whatismyip.com) or [IPInfo](https://ipinfo.io).
   3. Check the displayed IP address and location. If it matches your proxy server instead of your actual physical location, your proxy is working correctly. [4, 5, 6]
