1\. Base

Best for low fees

* Fast transactions.

* Generally low transaction fees.

* Good for frequent transfers.

![Ethereum Logo SVG and PNG Icons for Your Projects](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/SBLlsemg84irvTL48tChARF7Pvkfz46J6EG3r-psgqP7cA0-RjXJcX2EnIf9D2mM664i_nZSNReZqoYCOGOBo1j4AeLYT5CquCQnkysHR78cwf0Ao_6BTU2akCUl4GZLtysiq_nXm1P_1gj92thwE0tfS8NOxm_2rmY2zr30aZARF5C_E051h6bJGyekd16S?purpose=fullsize)

2\. Ethereum

Best for broad compatibility

* Widely supported.

* Established ecosystem.

* Usually higher transaction fees.

![Jobs | Solana Network Opportunities Job Board](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/5TsNe9NUVtaL2LdftGSvNFW1x6E109NHI7_zlflH5E2dItVxVibs1ViUfqwreIkoBAcUq5KrSYBOe3yEDH7GNXK91wUrSZ1OBgLLO7VupVtgtCkdfXu_4MhqS5OAX5qT6X-h8h9tD6oM1zol5YYDAWkc2wTl8F0enQOqJmX5q0zruxIqtTkOalRslfMAE7Ml?purpose=fullsize)

3\. Solana

Best for speed and low fees

* Very fast transfers.

* Generally inexpensive.

* Requires a Solana-compatible wallet.

My overall recommendation: Base, if your receiving wallet supports USDC on Base.

Remember: the network you select on HackenProof must exactly match the network used by your receiving wallet.
